# Identity and scribe evidence for Immanuel ben Gad de Milhaud

The correct Wikidata person item is Q132798628. It gives the name `ʻImanuel bar Gad di Millau` and cites the Columbia catalogue for the title page of volume 37. Columbia uses the same name for the title page and the second image. Both records name the same manuscript, CLIO 9160970, shelfmark X893C J55 v.37, dated 1689. The project's account did not create this person item. [Wikidata person](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q132798628) · [Columbia title page](https://online-exhibitions.library.columbia.edu/exhibits/show/hebrew_mss/item/7261) · [Columbia second image](https://online-exhibitions.library.columbia.edu/exhibits/show/hebrew_mss/item/7263)

The external name authority is Digital Scriptorium Q75810, with catalogue ID `DSNAME8399`. It is an instance of Personal (Name Authority) and links back to Q132798628. Wikidata links back through `P13311`. The Columbia catalogue supplies the source evidence for the name and the manuscript role. The authority cross-link identifies the person across the two systems. [Digital Scriptorium person authority](https://catalog.digital-scriptorium.org/wiki/Item:Q75810) · [Wikidata property P13311](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P13311)

The British Library records Gad ben Emmanuel Milhaud as the scribe of Or 10743. The colophon gives the reverse patronymic and dates the copy to 1729–1730. HUC Ms. K. 22, Q141747013, has the reverse patronymic in MARC 700 and names Immanuel ben Gad in field 957. This case is outside the 29 Immanuel cases and needs a check of the source manuscript before a repair. ## Source checks for the 29 cases

The full MARC records support 28 of the 29 Immanuel scribe cases through a signature or an explicit catalogue credit. They include the signature in field 957 for Columbia volume 16 and the credit in field 500 for volume 37. The primary Columbia catalogue confirms the title page for volume 37.

## Independent Penn evidence

- Penn names Immanuel as the copyist of CAJS Rar Ms 498 on 7 June 1663. The source colophon is at folio 69v. Penn gives the Hebrew name `דמילייאב, עמנואל בן גד` as a contributor. [Penn Ms. 498](https://find.library.upenn.edu/catalog/9977524315703681)
- Penn credits Immanuel for CAJS Rar Ms 500, made in 5480, or 1719–1720. The role for Yosef de Monteles is former owner. [Penn Ms. 500](https://find.library.upenn.edu/catalog/9977524315403681)
- Penn gives a probable attribution for CAJS Rar Ms 486. The manuscript lacks the beginning and the end. That copy provides context for style and manuscript production. [Penn Ms. 486](https://find.library.upenn.edu/catalog/9977505021403681)
