## Manuscript evidence

The full MARC field 957 supports Delfogit's scribe role on 20 of the 21 manuscripts. It includes the Venasque colophon for Columbia General 60 and the Carpentras colophon at folio 38v for Paris Ms. 31. The Günzburg 650 note gives a catalogue attribution. The Paris Ms. 32 note uses the spelling `אליהו קאלפוגייט`. The colophon for Columbia vol. 6 names Milhaud. Field 561 gives the relation to Delfogit's exemplar.

The Penn catalogue links three signed manuscripts. Its record for CAJS Rar Ms 491 gives the name Eliyahu Dalfoget and the date 1663. The same record connects him to the 1650 signed copy in Hamburg and the 1659 signed copy in Parma. It cites the Parma catalogue entry 1111 for the spelling Delpuget. The public Penn record and the related Digital Scriptorium items give identifiers for the manuscript and its catalogue record. They provide no person identifier.
