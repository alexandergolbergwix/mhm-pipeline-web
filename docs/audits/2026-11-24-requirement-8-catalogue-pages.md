# Catalogue pages for requirement 8

Each proposed source link identifies a description of the physical manuscript in the cited edition. The full MARC 581 field supplies the citation. The MARC 942 fields supply the shelfmark and, where available, the entry number or prior shelfmark. The proof record binds the exact citation text to the MARC occurrence, the Wikidata item, and the catalogue page.

The proof set contains 49 cases across four editions.

## British Museum catalogue

The 1899 and 1905 volumes and the 1909 and 1915 sections of Margoliouth's catalogue give the entry number and shelfmark on the same page. The proof uses the corresponding BookReader page index. The 1899 scan gives Harley 5521 for entry 161. The 1915 scan gives Sloane 5242 for entry 1052, which matches the alternate shelfmark in MARC.
