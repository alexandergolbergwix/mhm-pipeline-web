## Cases with open questions

- The signature for Joseph al-Badihi in Q141747278 appears in a later hand. The catalogue allows the possibility that it copies an earlier inscription. [Person authority](https://teman.org.il/content/11037)
- The Qarh person article identifies a scribe of a 1671 siddur. Field 500 explicitly questions whether his signature belongs to Q141746964. [Person authority](https://teman.org.il/content/13012)
- Teman quotes the colophon of Q141175783. The existing Q141384820 has an exact HMO link that associates several people with the same short name. Its VIAF link needs an identity check. [Person authority](https://teman.org.il/content/12545) · [Existing item](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q141384820)
- The al-Uzani source associates JTS Ms. 8091 with the copyist, but its signature reverses two ancestors. That conflict remains in Q141746866. [Person authority](https://teman.org.il/content/11362)
- The four generations and role for Q141748187 are clear. The review keeps both surname readings for duplicate checks.
- Q141747509 names Maimon al-Musai as a patron in the source quotation. The person authority gives scribe or patron as alternatives. [Person authority](https://teman.org.il/content/12141)
- A community article quotes the full Q141747442 signature of Yeshuah ben David ben Solomon al-Surab. The searches found a different named person article and no exact dedicated person authority. [Community manuscript evidence](https://teman.org.il/content/14194)
- Teman documents the separate 1644 Sutro 84 copy. Q141748176 describes Sutro 83. The reviewed records have different film IDs.
- The 1699 al-Taam signature is clear in the MARC transcription. A named matching authority remains open.
- The short Saadia son of Meoded headings cannot distinguish several scribes in the same region and period. [Authority distinctions](https://teman.org.il/content/12521)

## Duplicate evidence

The duplicate review checked exact Hebrew and Latin name forms and Teman URL variants. It used native Wikidata search and WDQS. The writer repeats the name and URL checks before it creates a person. Each edit checks the exact live revision and the original creator of the manuscript.

## Shimon ben Yonah Mizrahi

The two Baghdad colophons give the same full name. The 1697 prayer book, `990001679060205171`, names him on the title page. The 1680 commentary, `990001679300205171`, names him on folio 196v. Field `700:1` identifies the copyist in both records.

The biographical passage identifies his father Yonah and the Barzani, Adoni, and Mizrahi family names. The Hamburg study publishes his signed monogram. [Pe’amim, page 67](https://files.ybz.org.il/periodicals/peamim/13/Article_13.3.pdf#page=10) · [Hamburg manuscript study, figure 6](https://www.csmc.uni-hamburg.de/publications/mc/files/articles/mc09-2-3-wandrey.pdf)

The HMO person record links the two copies and lists life dates that conflict with both signed dates. The repair uses the source to identify the scribe and preserves the manuscript dates. The duplicate check includes the HMO entry. [HMO record](https://mhm-hmo.wikibase.cloud/wiki/Item:Q13051)

Both manuscript claims use the same person item. [Wikidata person item](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q142259895)
