# Evidence for the open scribe cases

The production audit `b40f5264-f156-41f6-a698-8da3ef273060` left 388 scribe cases open. The full MARC records contain 275 distinct normalized scribe names. The scribe entries have 17 `$d` date fields and no recognized authority identifier in `$0` or `$1`. The records contain 16 notes that explicitly mention a colophon. The occurrence can identify the role even when the authority link needs more evidence. [MARC 700](https://www.loc.gov/marc/bibliographic/bd700.html)
