## Moses al-Qati‘i

The colophon for Ms. Heb. 9913=28 names Moses son of Saadia son of Judah son of David al-Qati‘i. Other copies name the same scribe with a shorter lineage. The Hebrew Wikipedia sitelink for Q108378115 identifies Moses son of Saadia son of Judah. [Ktiv record](https://www.nli.org.il/he/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts/itempage?docId=PNX_MANUSCRIPTS990000558270205171&scope=KTIV&vid=KTIV), [Wikidata item](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q108378115), [Hebrew sitelink](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%94_%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%99)

The younger Moses also signed manuscripts. His colophons name his father as Moses and his grandfather as Saadia. The copies below identify Saadia as the scribe's father. [Father's transcribed colophons](https://teman.org.il/content/13032), [Additional colophons](https://teman.org.il/content/13033), [Son's transcribed colophons](https://teman.org.il/ar/content/13031)

## Joseph al-Adawi

All four colophons name Joseph son of Amram in Amran. The copies identify the al-Adawi family and give a longer lineage through Oded. A separate copy names Jacob son of Isaac son of Oded. [Ktiv record for Ms. Heb. 9963=4](https://www.nli.org.il/he/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts/itempage?docId=PNX_MANUSCRIPTS990001366470205171&vid=MANUSCRIPTS), [Joseph's transcribed colophons](https://teman.org.il/content/12565), [Jacob's transcribed colophon](https://www.teman.org.il/content/12568)

VIAF cluster `68156495355517561081` currently points to `J9U|987007402783005171`. The authority record names Amram son of Joseph. The order of the personal names conflicts with the colophons. Q141175696 names the scribe with the same lineage as the colophons. [VIAF cluster](https://viaf.org/viaf/68156495355517561081), [Wikidata item](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q141175696)

The first revision of Q141175696 gives the project creator ID `6935067`. [First person revision](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?oldid=2536220605)

## Ḥayim al-Hagagi

The British Library describes the signed colophon on folio 96v of Or. 2218. It names Ḥayim son of Shalom son of David son of Yitsḥaḳ and gives further generations and the family name. Q141953242 gives the same name in Hebrew. [British Library catalogue](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003376507), [Ktiv record for Or. 2218](https://www.nli.org.il/he/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts/itempage?docId=PNX_MANUSCRIPTS990001211120205171&vid=MANUSCRIPTS), [Wikidata item](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q141953242)

The NLI ID `987007312320105171` on Q141953242 resolves through VIAF to Yosef Yitsḥaḳ Shelush, 1891–1960. That authority identifier conflicts with the 17th-century colophon. The transcribed colophons give the same father and grandfather in other witnesses. [VIAF source record](https://viaf.org/viaf/sourceID/J9U%7C987007312320105171), [Transcribed colophons](https://www.teman.org.il/content/11428)

The first revision of Q141953242 gives the project creator ID `6935067`. [First person revision](https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?oldid=2563125514)

## Identity still unresolved

Three Florence records identify Don Cesare Mainardo as a scribe in 1609. MARC record `990001905840205171` connects the other two copies, `990001905850205171` and `990001905860205171`, to the same scribe. The exact-name candidate Q20557810 identifies a 20th-century poet. Q114837478 identifies a present-day executive. The source records support the historical scribe credit. An exact Wikidata person match remains open. [Study of the Florence copies](https://theo-logos.pl/server/api/core/bitstreams/195130fe-fe71-47e8-bf34-a513e0e0f9b7/content), [Q20557810 sitelink](https://pms.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cesare_Mainardi), [Q114837478](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q114837478)

## Mehler and Bashiri

- The full MARC notes for Q141746951 and Q141748072 name Judah Mehler Reutlingen in Bingen. One note gives his father's name, Samuel, and his place of birth, Fulda. Q1710859 points to GND 136768687. The other Judah Mehler, Q94782139, was born in 1660. The two copies date to the mid-17th century before that birth. [Deutsche Biographie](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz59820.html)
- Q141175902 has a 1618 MARC colophon that names Avner bar Ner ha-Sharoni. The Library of Congress authority for Yaḥya Bashiri, no2008160185, records this pen name. Q22935567 carries that authority ID. The exact repair updates the old Hebrew name qualifier to the current MARC heading. [Library of Congress](https://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/no2008160185.html)
