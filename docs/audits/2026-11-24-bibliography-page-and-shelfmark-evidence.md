## Cases that need more evidence

- The audit has 36 citations to the 1994 Bodleian supplement. The Bodleian distinguishes that edition from its original catalogue. The available copy has access restrictions. [Bodleian catalogue guide](https://libguides.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/c.php?g=422971&p=2888528) [1994 supplement](https://archive.org/details/catalogueofhebre0000bodl)
- The audit has 12 citations to the 1966 Brinner catalogue. The Library of Congress guide identifies the publication. The available copies have access restrictions. [Library of Congress guide](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/gdc/gdclccn/81/00/63/06/81006306/81006306.pdf) [Brinner catalogue](https://archive.org/details/sutrolibraryhebr0000unse)
- Some citation differences store a fragment such as a volume and page. The complete MARC occurrence and the cited page are necessary before a repair can use such a fragment.
