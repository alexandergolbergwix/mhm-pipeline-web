# Inscription scope and exact quotation proof

The completed audit left 433 open inscription cases. The review compared each original field with the public item for the exact Ktiv ID. Of these cases, 237 pointed to items with one active inscription, 182 pointed to items with more than one, and 14 pointed to items with none. The review checked quotation boundaries, inscription role, folio, and the Ktiv reference. ## Cases that need more evidence

The text comparison found 15 normalized quotation matches. Twelve of those cases need review for a location qualifier, language tag, or creator. Eight use a page number in the folio property. Two use a prose location. One has a Judeo-Arabic passage with a Hebrew language tag. Another item belongs to a different creator. The review keeps those cases open with their original source fields.

The original Columbia catalogue confirms two passages for Q141747678. It identifies the earlier passage as title-page text and the other passage at the end of the manuscript. Ktiv calls the earlier passage a colophon and gives a different editorial reading of the year. Both descriptions identify the same manuscript by shelfmark. The location and date discrepancy needs review.
